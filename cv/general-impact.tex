\cvsection{Selected Research Contributions and Impact}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Efficient AI models and product translation.} At Samsung AI Center, developed DictFormer (ICLR 2022), a compact Transformer architecture that was patented, demonstrated, and translated into Samsung products.
\item \textbf{Adaptive AI systems.} R2-Router (ICML 2026) uses reasoning to select models for requests. HW-Router (DAC 2026) brings hardware awareness to multi-model serving. Open-source releases support further research and evaluation.
\item \textbf{Secure and trustworthy AI.} TrojLLM (NeurIPS 2023) exposed automated prompt-injected Trojan risks in LLMs. DataSeal (IEEE S\&P 2025) supports verification of encrypted computation. FHE-Coder (ICLR 2026) helps generate cryptographic programs under functional and security checks.
\end{itemize}
