\cvsection{Selected Research Contributions and Impact}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Capable and efficient models.} DictFormer and weighted low-rank compression (ICLR 2022) make model capabilities smaller and more reusable, providing foundations for specialized agents. DictFormer was patented and translated into Samsung products.
\item \textbf{Collaborative intelligence.} R2-Router (ICML 2026) chooses models and output budgets to balance answer quality and cost. Gated-memory routing (EMNLP 2026) and latency-aware orchestration (NeurIPS 2026) study what agents retain and how they coordinate. \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.11440}{INFRAMIND} and \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.09613}{AgentServeSim} (preprints) connect collaboration to serving conditions and simulation-based policy search.
\item \textbf{Trustworthy collaboration.} MemIncept (ICML 2026) and conjunctive prompt attacks (ACL 2026) expose risks in agent memory and interaction. Clinical-text studies (EMNLP 2025/2026) examine factuality and watermarking. SecureRoute, DataSeal, and FHE-Coder address private computation and external checks of supported results and programs.
\item \textbf{Healthcare as an integrating application (ongoing).} The NIH/NIAID R01 studies knowledge-grounded collaborative agents for bacterial infection decision support. The goal is to connect specialized capabilities, evidence coordination, and reliability evaluation to clinical practice guidelines. Clinical benefits remain to be validated, with clinician oversight essential.
\end{itemize}
