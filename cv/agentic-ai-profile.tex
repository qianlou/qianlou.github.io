\cvsection{Research Profile}
Qian Lou is a researcher and professor at the University of Central Florida, serving as Principal Investigator on NSF-, NIH-, and Amazon-supported projects. His research connects capable and efficient models, collaborative intelligence, and trustworthy collaboration to expand what AI agents can accomplish together. He develops model architectures, routing and orchestration methods, and safeguards for agent interactions and computation. His NIH-supported research studies knowledge-grounded collaborative agents for healthcare decision support. He received the \textbf{NSF CAREER Award in 2026} and previously worked as a Senior Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center.
