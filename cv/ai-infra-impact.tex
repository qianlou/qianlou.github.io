\cvsection{Selected Research Contributions and Impact}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Efficient model architectures and algorithms.} DictFormer and weighted low-rank compression (ICLR 2022) reduce model size through shared representations and factorization. AutoQ (ICLR 2020) studies automated quantization; Lite-MDETR (CVPR 2022) extends compact model design to multimodal detection. DictFormer was patented and translated into Samsung products.
\item \textbf{Mixture-of-model serving and runtime control.} HW-Router (DAC 2026) brings hardware awareness to multi-model serving. R2-Router (ICML 2026) selects models and output budgets; Chain-of-Route and latency-aware orchestration (NeurIPS 2026) study stateful routing and multi-agent execution.
\item \textbf{Infrastructure-aware control and simulation.} \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.11440}{INFRAMIND} and \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.09613}{AgentServeSim} (preprints) connect execution decisions to queueing, cache state, and serving-policy search. AgentServeSim is evaluated against real vLLM deployments.
\item \textbf{Hardware-aware, reliable execution.} HBM-CASO (ISCA 2026) coordinates error correction for high-bandwidth memory. CipherFlow (NeurIPS 2026) plans hybrid secure inference with hardware and network awareness. ReliaFHE (ASPLOS 2026) and MATCHA (DAC 2022) address resilience and efficiency in encrypted-computing accelerators.
\end{itemize}
