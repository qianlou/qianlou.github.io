\cvsection{Research Profile}
Qian Lou is a researcher and professor at the University of Central Florida, serving as Principal Investigator on NSF-, NIH-, and Amazon-supported projects. His research co-designs models, runtime systems, and hardware to build efficient, scalable, and reliable AI infrastructure. He develops compact model architectures, mixture-of-model routing and serving, and hardware-aware execution systems, aiming to turn advances in AI capabilities into practical services with manageable computation and memory costs. He received the \textbf{NSF CAREER Award in 2026} and previously worked as a Senior Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center, where his model research contributed to patents and product translation.
