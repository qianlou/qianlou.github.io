\cvsection{Research Profile}
Qian Lou is a researcher and professor at the University of Central Florida, serving as Principal Investigator on projects supported by NSF, NIH, and Amazon. His research develops AI infrastructure for secure and private computation, private verification, and privacy-preserving learning and data synthesis. He has developed cryptographic primitives and compilers that enable new AI applications, and develops AI agents for cryptanalysis and cryptographic programming. He received the \textbf{NSF CAREER Award in 2026} and previously worked as a Senior Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center.
