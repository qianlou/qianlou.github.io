\cvsection{Research Profile}
Qian Lou is a researcher and professor at the University of Central Florida, serving as Principal Investigator on NSF-, NIH-, and Amazon-supported projects. His research exposes vulnerabilities in AI systems and develops defenses, evaluation methods, and privacy-preserving systems. His work spans prompt and memory attacks, certified robustness, model and text provenance, and verifiable computation. He aims to make increasingly capable and agentic AI more secure, accountable, and dependable through empirical evaluation and explicit security assumptions. He received the \textbf{NSF CAREER Award in 2026} and previously worked as a Senior Research Scientist at Samsung AI Center.
