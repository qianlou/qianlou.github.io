\cvsection{Research Profile}
Qian Lou is a researcher and professor building efficient, adaptive, and trustworthy AI systems through the co-design of models, computing infrastructure, and security. His work spans model innovation, dynamic multi-model execution, and secure and verifiable computing, with impact from open-source research to industrial products. He serves as Principal Investigator on NSF-, NIH-, and Amazon-supported projects, received the \textbf{NSF CAREER Award in 2026}, and brings experience from both academia and Samsung AI Center.
